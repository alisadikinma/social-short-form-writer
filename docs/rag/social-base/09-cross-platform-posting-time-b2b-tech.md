# 09 — Cross-Platform Posting Times (B2B Tech, Asia/Jakarta UTC+7, 2026)

**Summary:** For B2B tech in Asia/Jakarta (WIB), optimal posting windows are 07:00–09:00 and 12:00–13:00 on weekdays (Tue–Thu peak). TikTok adds a strong 20:00–22:00 evening window. Instagram and TikTok B2B content should post 3–5× per week; avoid weekends for professional/technical content.

## Baseline: Asia/Jakarta (WIB, UTC+7) Windows

Most global posting-time data is given in the creator/poster's local timezone. The data below has been converted to WIB (UTC+7) for an Indonesian operator posting to a globally distributed B2B tech audience that includes significant Southeast Asian + Asia-Pacific professional segments.

---

| Priority | Day | WIB Window | Rationale |

| High | Tuesday | 08:00–09:00 | Start-of-week decision-maker morning check |
| High | Wednesday | 08:00–09:00, 12:00–13:00 | Mid-week peak engagement, lunch break |
| High | Thursday | 08:00–09:00, 13:00–14:00 | Pre-weekend planning research |

**Frequency:** 2–3 posts per week for B2B carousel accounts. Multiple sources confirm that increasing beyond 3–5 posts/week for B2B does not improve reach if content quality dips (Buffer analysis of 2M+ posts, 2026). Quality > frequency.

**Frequency:** 3–5 posts per week for TikTok Photo Mode. TikTok rewards consistency in posting cadence more aggressively than Instagram (the algorithm uses posting frequency as a creator-reliability signal for FYP distribution).

## Rules

1. **Post in the 07:00–09:00 WIB window as the primary slot for both platforms.** This captures: Indonesian professionals starting their workday, APAC audiences in WIB+1 to WIB+3 starting mid-morning, and global audiences in US timezones waking up (EST = WIB - 12h, so 07:00 WIB = 19:00 EST the previous day — which is a secondary US evening engagement window).

2. **Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday are the B2B performance days for both platforms.** Multiple sources confirm weekday mid-week as peak for professional/technical content. Monday posts compete with weekend backlog; Friday posts lose attention after noon.

3. **Add an evening slot (20:00–22:00 WIB) for TikTok.** TikTok's FYP discovery skews toward evening more than Instagram. For Photo Mode carousels targeting practitioners, the evening slot can match or exceed the morning slot in reach.

4. **Never post both platforms at the exact same minute.** If cross-posting the same content, stagger by 30–60 minutes. This avoids a single traffic spike competing across platforms and allows each post to build its own engagement signal independently.

5. **Test and adjust based on audience analytics after 30 days.** All data above is baseline; your specific audience (Indonesian vs. global split, seniority of followers, niche sub-segment) may shift optimal times by 1–2 hours. Both platforms provide native analytics for best posting times once account reaches 100+ followers.

---

## Anti-Patterns

- Posting on Saturday or Sunday for B2B tech content — documented lower engagement quality for professional audiences
- Posting at 12:00 WIB sharp — peak competition slot; 11:50 or 12:10 reduces competitive noise
- Posting the same content on both platforms simultaneously — double-counts against your algorithm signals
- Posting more than once per day on Instagram — beyond 1/day, Instagram suppresses reach on subsequent posts within the same day
