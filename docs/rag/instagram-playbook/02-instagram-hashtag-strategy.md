## The Hard Cap (Not a Recommendation)

**Instagram enforced a 5-hashtag hard limit in December 2025.** This is not a best-practice suggestion — it is a platform constraint. Posts with more than 5 hashtags will have excess tags silently removed or will fail to publish. This applies to both captions and comments; splitting hashtags between caption and first comment does NOT grant extra slots beyond 5 total.

Source: Official Instagram @creators Threads post (December 2025) + Social Media Today coverage.

---

## Rules

1. **Use exactly 3–5 hashtags per carousel post.** Since the hard cap is 5, and data shows 3–5 relevant tags consistently outperform both 1–2 (under-categorized) and 5+ (spam-pattern, pre-cap behavior), use 3–5 as the default. For B2B tech carousels, 4 is the practical sweet spot: 1 broad + 2 niche + 1 format.

2. **Treat hashtags as content-categorization signals, not reach multipliers.** The 2026 Instagram algorithm uses hashtags primarily to route content to relevant interest graphs. A hashtag does not guarantee reach; relevance does. Choose tags that accurately describe your content's topic, not tags with the highest post count.

3. **B2B hashtag selection formula for AI/engineering content:**
   - 1 broad topic tag (1M–10M posts): `#AIEngineering`, `#ArtificialIntelligence`, `#SoftwareEngineering`
   - 2 niche tags (50K–500K posts): `#VibeCoding`, `#LLMDeployment`, `#AIAutomation`, `#AgenticAI`
   - 1 format or community tag (50K–500K posts): `#TechCarousel`, `#BuildInPublic`, `#StartupTech`

4. **Rotate your hashtag set across posts.** Using identical hashtag sets on every post triggers Instagram's spam-detection filter. Maintain 2–3 variations of your standard set and rotate them.

5. **Place hashtags at the end of the caption or in the first comment.** Both placements are equivalent in terms of reach (the 5-tag cap applies to both combined). End-of-caption is simpler to manage. First-comment placement keeps the caption visually cleaner but requires a third-party scheduler or quick post-publish action.

---

## Anti-Patterns

- Using more than 5 hashtags — now a hard platform violation, not just a style choice
- Using banned or restricted hashtags (e.g., `#boostfollowers`, `#likeforlike`) — silently suppresses the entire post without notification
- Using `#fyp`, `#viral`, `#explore` — these carry no categorization signal and waste one of your 5 slots
- Repeating the same hashtag set on every post without rotation — spam-detection trigger
- Using hashtags unrelated to the content to chase volume — sends incorrect categorization signals, reduces Explore fit
