## Key Difference From Pre-2025 TikTok Hashtag Advice

TikTok's algorithm updated in 2025–2026 to give more weight to caption keywords and video/slide content than to hashtags for FYP distribution. Hashtags still matter for **search indexing** and **initial content routing**, but they are no longer the primary lever for organic reach. Confirmed by TikTok's own creator guidelines: "#FYP, #ForYou, and #viral do not give your video a special boost in the algorithm."

---

## Rules

1. **Use 3–5 hashtags total using the pyramid method:**
   - 1–2 niche hashtags (under 50M total views on TikTok): `#VibeCoding`, `#LLMOps`, `#AIAgents`, `#AIEngineering`, `#AgenticAI`
   - 1–2 mid-tier hashtags (50M–500M views): `#SoftwareEngineering`, `#AITech`, `#TechTips`, `#Coding`
   - 0–1 broad hashtag (500M+ views): `#Tech`, `#AI`, `#Programming`

   Rationale: niche tags put you in a smaller competitive pool where you can rank at the top. Broad tags like `#tech` (billions of posts) make you invisible.

2. **Prioritize keyword-rich captions over hashtag volume.** In 2026, TikTok's search indexes your caption text with greater weight than hashtags for in-app discoverability. A caption that reads "How we run LLM evals before every production deploy — the 4-step framework" outperforms 10 hashtags on a generic caption in terms of search-driven reach.

3. **Never use #fyp, #foryoupage, or #viral.** These are confirmed no-ops for algorithmic boost (TikTok's creator guidelines explicitly state this). They waste a hashtag slot and signal low-effort content packaging.

4. **Match hashtags to the slide content, not to the broad topic area.** If the carousel is specifically about RAG architecture, use `#RAG` + `#VectorDB` + `#LLMDeployment` rather than `#AI` + `#ArtificialIntelligence` + `#Tech`. The algorithm routes based on the intersection of caption + hashtags + user behavior signals.

5. **Place hashtags at the end of the caption, not inline.** Inline hashtags (e.g., "This is why #VibeCoding is changing #SoftwareDevelopment") break the caption's readability and feel spammy on TikTok. End-of-caption placement keeps the hook clean and signals intentional categorization.

## Source Conflict Note

**Source A** (Sprout Social, TikTok creator guidelines): 3–5 hashtags is the recommended max.
**Source B** (skedsocial.com, Buffer): 5–8 hashtags acceptable for growth accounts.

**Resolution for B2B tech**: Lean toward 3–5. The lower end is appropriate for accounts targeting quality engagement over broad reach — a key B2B signal priority. 5–8 is defensible for discovery-phase accounts but carries spam-signal risk on TikTok's algorithm when quality falls.

---

## Anti-Patterns

- Using `#fyp`, `#foryou`, `#viral` — TikTok confirmed these have zero algorithmic boost
- Using 10+ hashtags — treated as spam-pattern on TikTok, different from pre-2024 advice
- Using hashtags that don't match the slide content — misroutes content to wrong interest graph
- Repeating identical hashtag sets on every post — reduces niche authority signal over time
- Using very high-view hashtags exclusively (billions of posts) — your content disappears in the noise
